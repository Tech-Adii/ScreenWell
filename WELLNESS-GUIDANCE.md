# Reminder guidance

Reviewed 2 November 2026. ScreenWell supplies optional comfort breaks, not treatment or vision correction. Its posture score is a personal-calibration heuristic. The below-80 alert threshold is a product preference, not a clinical cutoff.

Eye reminders offer six choices: distance gaze, soft blinking, closed-eye rest, shade without pressure, optional near/far viewing, and optional gentle figure-eight gaze. Users choose one; they need not complete all six. The last two are optional comfort movements, not exercises proven to correct eyesight. Stop movements that feel uncomfortable and look away from the display after reading the instructions.

The American Academy of Ophthalmology recommends regular look-away breaks and blinking for screen comfort: [Computers, digital devices and eye strain](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/computer-usage). This supports the distance-gaze and blink guidance; it does not validate every optional movement or the app's camera estimates.

Stretch reminders offer shoulder rolls, gentle neck tilt, chest opening, wrist flexor/extensor stretches and a supported change of position. Instructions avoid bouncing or forcing, and tell users to stop with pain. These are choices, not a requirement to complete a routine.

References: Mayo Clinic [desk stretches](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/in-depth/office-stretches/art-20046041), [wrist and forearm](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/multimedia/forearm-stretches/vid-20084698), and [neck stretches](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/multimedia/neck-stretches/vid-20084697).

Voice uses an installed English voice marked local by Windows/Chromium. Remote voices are excluded. No microphone or cloud speech service is used. Text remains available if speech is absent or playback fails.
